2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: email address, display name, username and an optional profile photo. Your display name doesn't have to be your legal name.
  • Age check: you enter your date of birth when you sign up. We use it once to confirm you're 18 or older and don't store it.
  • Agreements and preferences: which version of our Terms and this policy you accepted and when, whether you want product news by email, and your display and privacy settings.
  • Things you add yourself: property addresses, vehicle identification numbers (VINs), crypto wallet addresses, and items you value yourself such as art or watches.
  • Flexes and activity: your Flexes (posts), comments, the Flexes you flex (like), follows, blocks and reports you file.

Information from partners, with your permission

  • Identity verification: our verification partner confirms your legal name and that you're an adult. We keep your legal name and the result of the check. We don't keep images of your ID.
  • Financial accounts, through Plaid: when you link an account, Plaid gives us the account type, name, last four digits, balances, investment holdings, loan and card balances, and the name of the account owner (so we can confirm the account is yours). We never receive your bank username or password.
  • Property, vehicle and crypto values: our data partners estimate the value of property, vehicles and wallets you add, and check public records to confirm you own the property.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical and security data: device type, app version, IP address and error reports, used to keep the service secure and working.
  • Analytics, only if you allow it: how the app is used, to improve it. This is off unless you turn it on. No analytics tool is installed today.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your bank login, your full account numbers, payment card details (Wealthy is free), your precise location (GPS), your contacts, or advertising identifiers. After we use your home address to confirm which city and state you're ranked in, we keep only the city, metro area, state and country, not the street address.

3. How we use it

  • To calculate your net worth (verified assets minus debts) and your ranks.
  • To show your profile, posts and leaderboard entry, exactly as your settings allow.
  • To confirm your identity and age, prevent fake or duplicate accounts, and stop fraud and abuse.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and respond to support requests.
  • To send service messages (such as security alerts) and, only if you opt in, product news.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

We use financial and identity information only to provide Wealthy to you. We don't use it to infer anything else about you, and we don't make decisions with legal effects about you, such as credit or lending decisions.

4. What other members can see, and the risks of sharing

You decide. You can show your net worth as an exact amount, a range or not at all; show your location as your city, state or country; and choose whether to appear on public leaderboards at all (you're off them until you turn this on). Your ranks for places more specific than your location setting are never shown to others. Other members never see your legal name, addresses, banks, account numbers, property details, vehicle details or wallet addresses.

Anything you choose to make public can be seen, copied, screenshotted and shared by other members and by anyone they show it to. We can't control what they do with it, and to the extent the law allows, we aren't responsible for it. Sharing information about your wealth can attract unwanted attention, so think carefully before showing an exact amount or your city. See our Terms of Service.
