## Not included

- **Gotham** is listed first in the font settings so that devices with Gotham installed show it, but the font file is not included. Gotham is a commercial typeface from Hoefler&Co. To ship it in the app or on the website, buy a license that covers web and app embedding, then add the files here. Until then, everyone sees Montserrat.
- **Testing tools** used during development (Playwright, axe-core) are not part of the site.

## The name "Wealthy"

Search the USPTO trademark database (and app store listings) for existing uses of "Wealthy" in finance and social apps before launch, and consider filing for the mark.
