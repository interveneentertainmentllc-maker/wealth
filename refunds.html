1. Wealthy is free

Creating an account, linking accounts, seeing your ranks, your profile and the feed are all free. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases, setup fees, service fees or hidden charges, and Wealthy doesn't collect payment card details.

2. Refunds

Because Wealthy never charges you, there are no payments to refund.

Wealthy will never ask you for money, a payment card, gift cards or crypto, by email, text, phone or in the app. If someone does, and says they're from Wealthy or that paying will raise your rank or verify your account, it's a scam. Don't pay. Report it to [SUPPORT EMAIL], and if you already paid, contact your bank or card issuer right away.

If we ever offer anything paid, we'll publish a new Refund Policy and update our Terms of Service first. Before you could be charged, we'd show you the full price, any taxes, how often you'd be billed and how to cancel, and you would have to agree. Nothing would ever be charged automatically, and this policy wouldn't limit any refund rights you have under the law.
