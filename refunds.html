Refund Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]. Version 1.0.

The short version

  • Wealthy is free today. Nothing costs money, and there are no hidden fees.
  • If we add paid features, you'll see the full price before you pay, and you can cancel anytime.
  • If we charge you by mistake, we refund you.

1. Wealthy is free today

Creating an account, linking accounts, seeing your ranks, your profile and the feed are all free. We don't charge setup, service or withdrawal fees, and we never charge you without your clear confirmation.

2. If we add paid features

We may later offer a subscription (such as Wealthy Premium) or one-time services (such as professional valuation of art or a private company stake). Before you pay, we'll show you:

  • the price, including any taxes;
  • how often you'll be charged, and that the subscription renews automatically until you cancel;
  • how to cancel; and
  • for a free trial, the date it ends. We'll remind you before a trial turns into a paid subscription.

We'll give you at least 30 days' notice before raising the price of a subscription you already have, and you can cancel before the new price applies.

3. Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel at any time, as easily as you signed up:

  • Bought in the app on iPhone or iPad: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Wealthy.
  • Bought in the app on Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → Wealthy.
  • Bought on our website: Wealthy → Settings → Subscription → Cancel, or email [SUPPORT EMAIL].

When you cancel, you keep paid features until the end of the period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

4. Refunds

Purchases through Apple or Google

Apple and Google process these payments, so refunds are handled by them under their own policies. You can request one through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, or through Google Play's order history. We can't issue these refunds directly, but we're happy to help: email [SUPPORT EMAIL].

Purchases on our website

  • New subscriptions: if you ask within [14] days of your first payment, we'll refund it in full.
  • Valuation services: you get a full refund if you cancel before the valuation starts, or if we can't complete it.
  • Mistakes: if we charge you in error, charge you twice, or can't provide what you paid for, we'll refund the charge.

To request a refund, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] with the email address on your account and the date of the charge. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

5. Your legal rights

This policy doesn't limit any refund or cancellation rights you have under the law where you live.
