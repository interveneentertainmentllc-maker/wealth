Cookie Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]. Version 1.0.

The short version

  • Wealthy stores a few things on your device so the app works, like your settings.
  • We don't use advertising cookies or let other companies track you through Wealthy.
  • Optional analytics stays off unless you allow it. No analytics tool is installed today.

1. What cookies and device storage are

Cookies and similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, let a website or app save small pieces of information on your device. Wealthy uses local storage rather than cookies for everything listed below.

Wealthy's font is served from our own site, and the app doesn't load content from advertising networks or social media platforms. No third-party cookies are set.

3. Your choices

  • In the app: Settings → Legal → Cookie choices, or the "Allow analytics" switch in Settings. "Essential only" and "Allow analytics" are equally easy to choose, and you can change your mind at any time.
  • Global Privacy Control: if your browser sends this signal, we treat it as "Essential only" automatically.
  • In your browser: you can clear site data at any time. Clearing essential storage resets the test app and signs you out of the full service.

4. Changes

If we start using a new type of cookie or storage, we'll update this policy and, if it's optional, ask for your permission before using it.
